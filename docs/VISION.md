# Visual design brief

**Atmosphere:** grounded Scandinavian harbour at night in cold rain. Industrial, tense and believable rather than gratuitously neon. The player infiltrates a port with weathered containers, a cargo vessel, cranes, warehouses and security systems.

**World:** real-scale quay infrastructure: bollards, mooring lines, drain grates, cables, fences, rail tracks, forklifts, signage and utility equipment; plausible corrosion and dirt distribution, not repeated untextured blocks.

**Materials:** physically based metallic-roughness workflow; metal paint, weathered concrete, puddles, glass and rubber at coherent texture density. Use roughness/normal details and restrained reflections.

**Lighting:** cloudy moonlight, warm local floodlights, attenuation, shadows, cold rain and atmospheric fog; keep the whole level readable and performant.

**Viewmodel and NPCs:** detailed rigged weapon/hands with correct first-person perspective, convincing ADS, recoil and reload. Credible clothing, animation and enemy reactions.

**Level:** three sabotage objectives, followed by extraction; maintain navigation clarity and gameplay while increasing fidelity.

**Proof:** capture screenshots of the actual running game from fixed positions: vista, close-up, combat and landscape mobile. Report browser, GPU, resolution and measured FPS. The supplied promotional screenshots are an aspiration, not assets to copy.

**Assets:** provenance, author and licenses must be documented before including any external model, sound or texture.
